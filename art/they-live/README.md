# They Live — Jessie noir poses

Pulp-fiction detective noir cartoon sheets of Jessie, 48 poses in 3 sets of 16. No text on any sheet. Order in every sheet is top row left to right, then bottom row left to right.

## Sets

- `no-glasses-01-08.webp` — 1 happy, 2 angry, 3 sad, 4 surprised, 5 scared, 6 determined, 7 confused, 8 proud
- `no-glasses-09-16.webp` — 9 disgusted, 10 bored, 11 excited, 12 worried, 13 sly, 14 exhausted, 15 curious, 16 calm
- `glasses-lowered-01-08.webp` — same 1–8, black sunglasses lowered, eyes over the top, like the They Live photo
- `glasses-lowered-09-16.webp` — same 9–16, glasses lowered
- `glasses-worn-01-08.webp` — same 1–8, black sunglasses actually worn, fully covering the eyes
- `glasses-worn-09-16.webp` — same 9–16, sunglasses actually worn

## Known flags

- `glasses-lowered-01-08.webp`: he is holding the glasses down by his chin in most panels, not lowered on his face like the reference photo. The 09–16 lowered sheet has the pose right.
- `glasses-worn-01-08.webp`: background neon signs carry gibberish lettering. On surprised / scared, the eyes show through the lenses wide.
- `glasses-worn-09-16.webp`: on excited / curious, the eyes show through the lenses. Curious (15) has a magnifying glass prop.
- The v1 sheets (labelled, overacted expressions) are not in this branch — rejected, not included.
