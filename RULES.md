# Rules

## Rule 1: Real fruit, real nature. AI only for science.

- AI images are only for science: cells, molecules, skin layers, crystals, bacteria, anything a camera can't see.
- Fruit, plants, flowers, the farm, nature, and product jars are always real photos from FlashTown and Rooster Island.
- Never fake fruit. Never fake nature. Never fake a jar.
- No screenshots or photos from other people's pages. Only our own shots.
- Characters (Ipo, Dinny, the bots) are drawn art and are fine.

## Names

- Ipo and Erv are character names, written Ipo and Erv. They are not process names.
- Call processes by their science names: freeze-thaw cell rupture, crystalline lipid fat matrix, emulsification, fermentation.

## The science (what Ipo can say)

- Fruit is grown at FlashTown and Rooster Island, picked and processed the same day.
- Picked fruit is still alive: it keeps respiring and loses vitamin C over time. Freezing stops the clock.
- Freeze-thaw cell rupture: ice crystals puncture the cell walls, and the contents leak out when it thaws. Ice freezes pure water first, so the first drip is the most concentrated (freeze concentration).
- Aloe leaves stand for an hour to drain the latex (aloin). What's left is clear gel with its acemannan chains intact.
- The thawed slurry is warmed on crock pot low, mainly to match the temperature of the melted butters, then emulsified with soy lecithin and homogenized.
- Shea, mango, and cocoa butter plus 92 degree coconut oil set into a crystalline lipid fat matrix. Coconut (92F / 33C) and cocoa (about 34C) melt right around skin temperature.
- Leucidal Complete Plus is a ferment filtrate (Lactobacillus and Leuconostoc ferments, bacteria filtered out). It is an antimicrobial.

## Words to avoid

- "Probiotic" for Leucidal. Say "fermented" or "ferment filtrate."
- "No preservatives." Say "no synthetic preservatives."
- "No heat." Say "never cooked."
- "Structured water."
- Any claim about ice cream bean on skin. There is almost no research. Talk about the plant, the farm, and nitrogen fixing.
- Lilikoi moisture study was oral (eaten), not topical. Say "packed with antioxidants," not "proven to moisturize."
